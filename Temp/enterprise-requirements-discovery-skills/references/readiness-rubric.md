# Readiness and ambiguity heuristic

For each applicable domain (business goals, users, workflows, functional scope, data/integrations, security/compliance, nonfunctional needs, delivery, acceptance): rate 0 = unknown/conflicted, 1 = partial, 2 = clear and supported by user evidence.

Coverage = sum(scores) / (2 × number of applicable domains). Indicative ambiguity = 1 - coverage. Treat this as a discovery prioritization aid, not a quality certification. Report unknowns and conflicts alongside any number. A project with critical unresolved compliance, ownership, or core workflow decisions is NOT implementation-ready regardless of average score.

Completion states: Discovery in progress; Draft-ready with open questions; Stakeholder-review-ready; Approved for handoff (requires explicit stakeholder approval).



\## Readiness communication rules



\- Do not calculate or announce readiness during ordinary conversations.

\- Provide readiness assessments only when requested or during explicit project finalization.

\- Keep normal progress updates to one confirmed fact and one key gap.

\- Always identify critical unresolved decisions.

\- Never interpret a numerical score as automatic approval.
