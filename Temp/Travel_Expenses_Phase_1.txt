# Project Orion: Travel and Expense Platform

## Overview
Project Orion is a fictional internal platform that lets employees book business travel and submit expense reports in one place. Its goal is to cut expense approval time from 10 days to 2 days. The project sponsor is the Chief Financial Officer, and the delivery lead is Priya Nair.

## Scope
In scope:
- Travel booking for flights, hotels and rental cars.
- Expense report submission with receipt photo upload.
- Manager approval and finance review workflow.
- Monthly spend reports by department.

Out of scope:
- Payroll integration.
- International tax filing.

## Key Facts
- Target go-live date: 15 March 2027.
- Pilot group: 120 employees in the Sales and Operations departments.
- Budget: USD 480,000.
- Receipts above USD 75 require a photo attachment.
- Manager approval is needed for expenses above USD 250.
- Finance reviews every expense above USD 2,000 within 24 hours.
- The system runs on a cloud-hosted web app with single sign-on.

## Roles and Responsibilities
- Employee: books travel and submits expenses.
- Manager: approves or rejects expenses above USD 250.
- Finance reviewer: audits high-value expenses and policy exceptions.
- Administrator: manages policy rules and the list of approved vendors.

## Known Requirements
1. Employees must be able to submit an expense from a mobile phone.
2. Managers must receive an email notification within 5 minutes of a submission.
3. Rejected expenses must show a reason code.
4. All approvals must be kept for 7 years for audit purposes.
